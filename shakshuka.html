Shakshuka recipe

Return to recipe homepage

Description

Shakshuka is a delicious mediterranean tomato-based dish that is easy and quick to cook. I love this recipe because it can be cooked in a single pan, making clean up a breeze.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 1 medium bell pepper, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 can of crushed tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 medium lemon, juiced
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon salt, add more to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 6 large eggs
  • 3 tablespoons coarsely chopped parsley and/or cilantro
  • 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese
  • pita bread for serving

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add in onion and bell pepper. Cook until onion is translucent. Add in garlic and tomato paste. Cook until tomato paste darkens in color slightly.
  2. Add cann of crushed tomato, water, lemon juice, cumin, paprika, salt, and black pepper. Stir until combined and simmer until sauce is thickened slightly, about 10 minutes.
  3. Make 6 wells with a large spoon in the sauce, spacing them evenly apart. Crack an egg into each well and season lightly with salt. Cover and cook for 3 to 4 minutes.
  4. Garnish with cilantro and/or parsley and feta cheese. Serve with pita.
