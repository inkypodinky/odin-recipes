Bacon recipe

Return to recipe homepage

Description

Bacon may not be a health food, but it is delicious. And like our other recipes, it is easy to make and easy to clean up! It's a great breakfast food for these reasons.

Ingredients

  • One pound of your choice of bacon
  • Roll of parchment paper

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to the temperature specified on the bacon packaging. This will usually be 400° Fahrenheit.
  2. In a flat sheet pan, lay out the parchment paper to completely cover the pan.
  3. Lay in the bacon on the pan and parchment paper. It is okay if the bacon is touching and bunched together. It will cook just fine like that.
  4. Pop the bacon in the oven. Cook for the amount of time specified on the packaging, but this is usualy about 20 minutes.
  5. Consider flipping the bacon halfway through the time, or rotating the sheet around in the oven so that everything is cooked evenly.
  6. Check bacon for doneness. Everyone has different preferences, so if you like your bacon crunchier, feel free to leave it in for longer. But make sure to check in regular intervals, such as every 5 to 10 minutes, so that you do not burn the bacon.
  7. There is not a specific need to check the bacon for temperature, but 165° Fahrenheit is recommended to ensure safety.
  8. Enjoy your bacon!
